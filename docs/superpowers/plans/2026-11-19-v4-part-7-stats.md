## What differs from the web, on purpose (said plainly to the owner)

- **The chart is drawn by hand** (react-native-svg), with the same curve type, ticks, margins and colours as the recharts one.
- **"Delete this weigh-in?" replaces the form inside the same sheet** (the web opens a second, centred dialog): one Modal at a time.
- **The date is the phone's date picker** (`DateField`), as Today's forms.
- **A card unfolds with Reanimated's layout transition** (the web animates height with framer-motion); none under Reduce Motion.
