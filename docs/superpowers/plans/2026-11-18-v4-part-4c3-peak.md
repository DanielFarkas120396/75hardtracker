# v4 Part 4c-3: Today, the day-complete peak and the finishing touches Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Finishing the fifth task ends on the web's "Day N done." peak: the world's sunrise, embers rising, the duck beside a stretch of the climb where today's stone lights up in gold, its line, the streak, "Nice!", with the chime. Plus the last pieces of the show: the weigh-in card folding away, the animated flame, the burst behind a sheet's done badge.

**Architecture:** `useDayCompleteCelebration` is the web's hook on the native gate, held at the Today route so it survives Today's own swaps; `useSettledCelebration` makes it wait for the board's last chip (5 s at most). The overlay is a full-screen view over the tabs, not a `Modal` (a sheet may still be leaving). Stones are react-native-svg, from `@logic/worlds`; embers and the sunrise are Reanimated views. The chime is rendered to a WAV by `mobile/scripts/render-sounds.mjs` from the web's recipe (`src/lib/sound.ts`) and played with `expo-audio`.

**Tech Stack:** as part 4c-2.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-expo-v4-design.md` (build step 4c). Web designs: `2026-10-11-day-complete-peak-design.md`, `2026-11-17-weigh-in-exit-design.md`, `2026-11-18-coherence-pass-design.md`. Every string: `today-porting-brief.md` sections 1.3, 5.9 and 6.1.

## What differs from the web, on purpose (said plainly to the owner)

- **The chime is a file** rendered from the web's recipe; nobody has heard it yet.
- **The flame** is the web's Lottie drawing only if its player is in Expo Go; otherwise it is redrawn (said in the report which).
- **A haptic** (success) when the peak opens: new in v4, as the design asks.
- On Day 75 the button reads "See your victory" and closes the overlay onto Today's plain "75 days done" screen until part 4d builds Victory.

## Global Constraints

- Parts 1 to 4c-2's constraints hold.
- The peak shows once per day and attempt: unticking and re-ticking a task doesn't bring it back; opening the app on a finished day never shows it; a day undone while it waited cancels it.
- It waits for the board's last chip, 5 s at most, and for the open sheet to have closed.
- Reduce motion: no embers, no burst, the sunrise there at once, today's stone already lit; the weigh-in card fades and the page moves up in one step.
- VoiceOver reads the overlay's words only (no stones, no duck, the streak once) and stays inside it.

## Review Focus

1. The fifth task from the board, and from a sheet: the peak appears after the chip lands and the sheet has gone; the chime plays once if sound is on.
