# v4 Part 4c-1: Today, the five fills Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On the phone, each open tile fills as its task progresses, as on the web: water's wave, diet's ink, workouts' sprint, photo's iris, reading's book. The status counts up with its fill, the fills stop under a sheet, take the world's and the theme's colours, and honour reduce motion.

**Architecture:** Each fill's timing is a model shared with the web (`src/logic/fillWave.ts`, `fillInk.ts`, `fillSprint.ts`, `fillIris.ts`, `fillBook.ts`: the web's painters now draw from them, PR 108). The phone draws a model's frame with one Skia fragment shader per fill (`mobile/src/today/fills/shaders.ts`), on one canvas per tile. A small engine (`engine.ts`) steps the models on the JS thread, as the web's does: only while something moves, fades or drifts, drift at 30 frames a second, nothing under a sheet. It writes each frame's uniforms into a Reanimated shared value that Skia reads on the UI thread.

**Tech Stack:** as part 4b, plus `@shopify/react-native-skia` 2.14.0 (in Expo Go for SDK 58).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-expo-v4-design.md` (build step 4c). Web designs: `2026-10-09-task-tile-fills-design.md`, `2026-11-17-book-page-fade` (in the journal), `docs/prototypes/README.md`. Algorithms: `today-porting-brief.md` sections 5.1 and 5.3 to 5.7.

## What differs from the web, on purpose (said plainly to the owner)

- **The book and the iris are drawn by a shader, not a 3D scene.** Skia has no meshes with a depth buffer or lights. The book's pages are traced per pixel along the same curl (`pageAngle`), with the same perspective, shading, front and back colours, and the nearest surface winning; the iris's blades are the same eight plates with the same lighting formula (three.js's standard material, written out). The numbers are the web's; the pixels come from different code. **The owner sees rendered images of both, beside the web's, before they count as settled.**
- **A tile off screen keeps its fill moving** (the web pauses it). The board is short; the loop still stops when nothing moves, under a sheet, and when Today is not the tab in front.
- **The fills are drawn at the phone's full pixel density** (the web caps at 2). To watch on the phone: no heat after a minute on Today.
- **A fill that can't be made** (the shader doesn't compile on this phone) leaves the thin bar, as on the web.

## Global Constraints

- Parts 1 to 4b's constraints hold (the `v4` worktree, `npm run check` before every commit, `@logic/*` and `@schema` only, no clock or ref read while rendering, tests outside `app/`).
- Same numbers as the web: every setting comes from the shared models; a shader takes its constants from them, never a second copy.
- The tile's text keeps 4.5:1 over its fill in every world and theme: the canvas's opacity is `fillOpacity(kind, palette)`, and over a fill the status line takes the ink colour.
- Reduce motion: a fill crossfades to its level in 0.25 s and nothing drifts.
- Jest has no GPU: the shaders are compiled and drawn in tests with CanvasKit (the same Skia, in WebAssembly), and their pixels checked. What only the phone can show (speed, heat, the look at full size) is listed for the owner.

## Review Focus

1. Opening Today with a day in progress shows every fill at its level at once; only a change animates.
2. "+ 250 ml" four times quickly: the level and the count end on 1 L; Undo takes one pour back.
3. A sheet open: nothing is stepped or drawn; what changed under it plays when it closes.
4. A new world or theme recolours every fill without restarting it.
5. Reduce motion: no rise, spread, run, iris or pages; a crossfade.
6. The status line over a fill is in ink and counts with it (water by 50 ml, pages one by one), then tells the day as it is.
7. Every shader compiles in CanvasKit and draws what its model says (empty at 0, covering at 1).

### Task 2: The engine

- `npx expo install @shopify/react-native-skia`; a Jest stand-in for its components.
- `mobile/src/today/fills/engine.ts`: `createFillEngine()` with `add(kind, surface, onFrame)` returning the web's `FillHandle` (`setLevel(level, 'animate' | 'fade' | 'instant')`, `shown`, `settledAt`, `setAnchor`, `setHasImage`, `setSize`, `remove`), `hold`, `setPalette`, `setReduceMotion`. Tests: the loop runs only while a fill moves, fades or drifts; drift is drawn at 30 frames a second; held, nothing is stepped; a fade reports settled only when it ends; a new palette redraws.

### Task 3: Sprint, wave, ink

`mobile/src/today/fills/shaders.ts`: the three web fragment shaders in SkSL (position from the tile's centre with y up, premultiplied output, no `discard`), each with the function that turns a model's frame into uniforms.

Tests (CanvasKit): each compiles; empty at level 0; the sprint covers the left and not the right at one half; the water covers the bottom and not the top; the ink covers the whole tile at 1 and starts from its anchor.

### Task 4: The tile

- `mobile/src/today/fills/TileFill.tsx`: the canvas under the tile's content, made at the day's level, reporting `shown` in steps and `settledAt`.
- `TaskTile`: the fill in place of the thin bar when its kind is available; the count-up (`countedData`), the status in ink over a fill, a text shadow in the canvas colour; the anchor measured from the quick chip or the switches.
- `TaskBoard`: one engine per board, held while a sheet is open or Today is not in front; the board's data frozen under a sheet (`useFrozenWhile`).

Tests, from `TaskTile.test.tsx` and `TaskBoard.test.tsx` with a fake engine: the count-up then the day as it is; the thin bar without an engine; the fills held under a sheet and the change played after.

### Task 5: The iris

The photo as a Skia image behind eight plates, in one shader: the duotone, the topmost plate per pixel with the web's stacking and tilt, the standard material's lighting, the edge lines, the flash. Shut until the photo is decoded (1 s at most).

Tests (CanvasKit): open at rest on the photo; shut, every pixel is a blade; the flash whitens.

### Task 6: The book

The fill, the open book's gutter and up to nine turning pages in one shader, each page traced along `pageAngle` with the web's perspective and shading.

Tests (CanvasKit): the fill covers a tenth per page with its feathered edge; the spine's shade comes with the first page; a page in mid-turn stands over the spine and shows its back once past it.
