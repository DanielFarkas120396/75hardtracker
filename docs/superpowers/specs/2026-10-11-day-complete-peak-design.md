# "Day complete!" peak (design)

Date: 2026-10-11 · Status: **built** (PR #82, on top of #79). Item 5 of the Today critique. The owner compared three takes in the app on 2026-10-11 (A Embers, B The stone, C The duck) and chose "combine A and B".

## Why

The critique found the peak was the most generic screen in the flow. It was a flat tan field, candy confetti and "Nice!", weaker than each task's own fill. The climb, the one thing only this app has, didn't appear on Today at all.

## What it is

The overlay opens over Today when the fifth task is done, as before:

- **The field** is the app's canvas (dark warm black in dark mode), not a flat world colour.
- **The world's sunrise** swells up behind the duck and the stones (A).
- **Embers** drift up from the bottom in the world's shades, instead of confetti (A). They rise twice, then they're gone.
- **A short stretch of the climb** sits beside the celebrating duck (B). It uses the Journey's own stones: two days behind, today, two ahead. Today's stone starts as "today" (its number and pulsing ring). A burst of the world's colour goes off around it, and it lights up as won (its tick). The days the streak covers are won; a day behind the streak shows as forgiven with a joker; the days ahead are locked. The stretch stops at Day 1 and Day 75.
- **The words:** "Day 12" in the display face, "done." under it in the world's ink, then "One more stone. 63 to go." Late logging keeps "Logged just in time. The streak lives. Now go get today." Day 75 says "That was the last one. All 75 days, every task, every day."
- **The streak** uses the app's flame, not an emoji.
- **The button** is the world-coloured primary pill, "Nice!" ("See your victory" on Day 75, without the 🏆). It takes focus when the overlay opens.

The sound plays as before. The victory screen keeps its confetti (in the world's colours since PR #79).

## Reduce motion

No embers and no burst. The sunrise is shown at once, and today's stone is already lit.
