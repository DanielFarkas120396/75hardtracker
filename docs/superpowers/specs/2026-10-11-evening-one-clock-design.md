# One clock in the evening (design)

Date: 2026-10-11 · Status: **built** (PR #80). Approved by the owner on 2026-10-10, as item 2 of the Today critique ("The duck, the hero's time left and the red tile edges all count to bedtime"; "keep it harsh").

## The problem

In the evening, Today showed three clocks at once:

- The duck threatens against bedtime (Settings → Companion, 23:00 by default).
- The hero's red line counted to midnight ("4h30 left").
- The red tile edges were judged per task against midnight.

So at 19:30 the duck could be hunting while the hero said "4h30 left".

## The rule

The evening has one deadline at a time:

- **Before bedtime**, it's bedtime.
- **Once bedtime has passed**, it's midnight, when the day itself ends.

Everything urgent reads that deadline:

- **The hero's line** says which clock it is: "1h30 to bedtime", then "45 min to midnight".
- **The red edges** keep their per-task rule against the new deadline. A tile is red when that task alone can't be finished before it. The other open tiles keep the grey ring.
- **The duck's "won't fit" line** says "Bedtime's coming. So am I." instead of "Midnight's coming". "Past your bedtime. Not mine." stays.

The duck's menace rule doesn't change. He stays harsh, and "Plan my evening" stays hidden while he hunts (owner's ruling).

## Left as is

- The hunting catchphrase "Midnight's coming. So am I." It's random chatter, and after bedtime it's true.
- Plans may still run until midnight (the plan sheet's limit).
